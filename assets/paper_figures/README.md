# Figures of the paper

The numbered figures of "Training Vision-Language Models with Margin-Based Resolution
Selection", cropped from the manuscript PDF without their captions at 200 dpi. Regenerate
them with `python scripts/extract_paper_figures.py /path/to/paper.pdf` (requires PyMuPDF).

| File | Caption as printed in the paper |
|---|---|
| `fig01.png` | Figure 1. Choosing a training resolution with RAT. For each receipt, the reader scores both candidate totals. RAT uses the lowest resolution at which each correct answer outscores its alternative by a sufficient margin. If neither resolution passes, RAT skips the pair. |
| `fig02.png` | Figure 2. Visual detail versus additional optimization. (a) Paired-token cosine dissimilarity at L and H. The scatter shows 1,050 pairs, the contours use all 4,800 pairs, and the dashed line marks equal dissimilarity. (b) Mean paired-accuracy gain H1× − L4× at fixed H test input, grouped by edit scale and initial loss. Diamonds mark H1× − U1× > 5 points. |
| `fig03.png` | Figure 3. Overview of Representation-Aware Training (RAT). Frozen encoders produce visual tokens and question-conditioned candidate embeddings. For each image and candidate, the reader pools the tokens with candidate-specific attention and scores the result by a scaled inner product with a learned candidate direction. The 2 × 2 score matrix gives the correct-answer margin for each image. RAT selects the mode with the fewest visual tokens whose smaller margin reaches the threshold and excludes pairs that fail in both modes. Each selected image contributes one teacher-forced example using its original visual features at the assigned mode. One pass of answer-only cross-entropy updates language-side LoRA within the shared compute budget, with the backbone and reader frozen. |
| `fig04.png` | Figure 4. Core controls on Qwen2.5-VL-3B (Table 4). (a–c) Filled bars show paired accuracy and outlined bars show paired accuracy on held-out renderings (mean ± sd, three seeds). Annotations give gains in paired accuracy over the other controls from left to right. Reader filters pairs at fixed H. Loss ranks pairs by initial answer loss. Student uses the initial student's candidate margin, and Mean and Min aggregate the two reader margins. Accuracy axes start at 70%. (d) Costs relative to all-H training on the same pairs. Labels give absolute means. |
| `fig05.png` | Figure 5. Threshold sensitivity on Qwen2.5-VL-3B. Points and error bars show means and standard deviations over three seeds. Lines connect the three evaluated thresholds. The shaded band marks the selected γ = log 4. Retention is measured over the 5,000-pair tuning pool. The H share is measured among retained pairs. Development accuracy is evaluated on 2,000 pairs at H. Trial cost excludes shared preparation. |
| `fig06.png` | Figure 6. Illustrative routing decisions on paired benchmark images. Each column pairs a benchmark image with a local edit under the same question and shows the relevant crops at low and high resolution. The routing decisions and the fixed-H answer rows for random allocation and RAT are hand-authored illustrations. Red marks answers that differ from the reference. |
